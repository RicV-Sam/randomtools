Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Spinnit (spinnit.site) is a fully static website. It runs entirely in your browser — there is no server, no database, and no backend. We have kept data collection to an absolute minimum.

What data we collect

We collect no personal data whatsoever. Spinnit does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any information. All tools run locally in your browser and nothing you enter is ever sent to a server.

Cookies

Spinnit currently sets no first-party cookies. We do not use session cookies, tracking pixels, or local storage for analytical purposes. If Google AdSense is activated in future (see below), third-party cookies will be set by Google — you will be informed via a cookie consent notice before any ads are displayed.

Google AdSense

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense in the future to help keep the site free. When active, Google AdSense may:

  • Set DoubleClick and other advertising cookies in your browser.
  • Use your browsing behaviour to serve personalised ads (where permitted by your consent choices).
  • Collect your IP address, browser type, and other technical identifiers.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Google Analytics

Spinnit does not currently use Google Analytics. If it is added in future, this policy will be updated and you will be notified via a consent banner. Any analytics implementation will respect your consent choices and applicable privacy law.

Third-party links

Some tool pages may contain links to external websites (for example, Wikipedia articles about countries or names). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external site you visit.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Spinnit is a general-purpose utility site suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK). Because we collect no personal data at all, Spinnit is safe for children to use. If you are a parent or guardian with concerns, please contact us at privacy@spinnit.site.

GDPR & UK GDPR rights

If you are located in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR, including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you.

Because Spinnit collects no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. However, if Google AdSense is enabled and you wish to exercise your rights regarding Google's processing of your data, please contact Google directly or use their privacy controls.

If you have any GDPR-related queries specific to Spinnit, email us at privacy@spinnit.site and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add advertising or analytics. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of Spinnit after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions, concerns, or GDPR requests? Reach us at privacy@spinnit.site.
