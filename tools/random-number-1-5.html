A number from 1 to 5 is perfect when you've got a short list of choices and can't make up your mind. Five options is the sweet spot — long enough to feel like a real decision, short enough that every answer has a meaningful 20% chance.

When to use 1–5

  • Picking one of five restaurants, films or takeaway options
  • Rolling a fair d5 in tabletop games that don't have a native five-sided die
  • Generating random star ratings for test data or mock reviews
  • Choosing a prompt from a list of five writing or drawing ideas
  • Assigning one of five coloured teams or groups

How to use

Tap GENERATE or press Space. Generate up to 100 at once by changing the count field. Pair numbers with your list of options in the order they appear.

Other ranges

Try 1–10, 1–2, 1–6, 1–100, or build a custom range.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is each number equally likely?

Yes — each of 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 has a 20% chance on every generation.

Can I use this as a d5?

Yes. A d5 is uncommon in physical dice sets, so most tabletop players roll a d10 and halve it. This generator is simpler — you get a fair 1-to-5 roll directly.

Can the same number repeat?

Yes. Each generation is independent, and with only five options repeats are common. That's expected and mathematically correct.
