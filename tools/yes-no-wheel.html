Yes / No Wheel

Spin the wheel — let fate decide

—

Tap spin to get your answer

The Yes No Wheel gives you a satisfying spinning animation that lands on either Yes or No — or Maybe if you prefer a three-way split. You can adjust the mode to weight the result towards one answer, which makes it more versatile than a simple coin flip. It's the most fun way to settle a quick decision or break a tie.

How to Use

  1. Choose your preferred mode from the dropdown (Yes/No, Yes/No/Maybe, Probably Yes, or Probably No).
  2. Click Spin the Wheel or tap the canvas directly.
  3. Wait for the wheel to slow and land on your answer.
  4. Spin again as many times as you like — there's no limit.

Common Uses

  • Deciding whether to order a takeaway or cook tonight
  • Breaking ties in group decisions where nobody can agree
  • Classroom activities for random student engagement
  • Daily decision challenges for content creators and streamers
  • Settling friendly arguments between colleagues or housemates
  • Kids' games and party activities where a fun spin adds to the atmosphere

Why Use Spinnit

The weighted modes — Probably Yes and Probably No — make this more versatile than a standard coin flip when you want a nudge in a particular direction. The wheel animation is smooth and responsive on all screen sizes. No installation, no log-in, and no data is ever sent anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add a Maybe option?

Yes. Select "Yes / No / Maybe" from the mode dropdown and the wheel will be divided equally into three segments.

Can I weight the result towards yes or no?

Yes. The "Probably Yes" and "Probably No" modes give a 70% chance to one outcome and 30% to the other, so the wheel still has some suspense.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up required.

Does this work on mobile?

Yes, all Spinnit tools are mobile-first and work on any device.
