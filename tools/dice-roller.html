The Dice Roller lets you roll any type of die — from a classic D6 to a D100 or any custom-sided die you define. Choose how many dice to roll at once and get your total instantly. Whether you're mid-session in a tabletop RPG or just need a quick random result, this tool has you covered.

How to Use

  1. Select your dice type from the dropdown (D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20, D100, or Custom).
  2. If you chose Custom, enter the number of sides you need.
  3. Set how many dice to roll at once (up to 20).
  4. Click Roll Dice, or use the quick-roll buttons for the most common types.
  5. Press Space to roll again instantly without using the mouse.

Common Uses

  • Dungeons & Dragons and other tabletop RPG sessions
  • Board games that need specific dice not in the box
  • Classroom probability or statistics demonstrations
  • Game night when physical dice are not to hand
  • Testing game mechanics and probability distributions
  • Resolving in-game decisions during video game playthroughs

Why Use Spinnit

All rolls happen locally in your browser — no app to install and no loading times. The tool supports up to 20 dice at once with an automatic sum total, making it ideal for RPG players who need fast results mid-session. No sign-up, no data stored, works on any device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I roll multiple dice at once?

Yes. Set the "Number of dice" field to up to 20 and the tool will show each individual result plus a running total.

What dice types are available?

D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20, and D100 are available as presets. You can also choose Custom and enter any number of sides from 2 to 1000.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up required.

Does this work on mobile?

Yes, all Spinnit tools are mobile-first and work on any device.
