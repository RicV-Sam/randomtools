Lottery Number Generator

Generate your lucky numbers for major lotteries worldwide

Tap generate to pick your numbers

The Lottery Number Generator creates sets of random numbers for the world's biggest lotteries — including UK Lotto, EuroMillions, Powerball, Mega Millions, and more. Select your lottery, choose how many lines you want, and get your numbers instantly. A custom mode lets you set your own rules for any lottery not already in the list.

How to Use

  1. Select your lottery from the dropdown menu.
  2. Set how many lines of numbers you want (up to 6 lines).
  3. Click Pick My Numbers to generate your selection.
  4. Main numbers appear in purple balls; bonus balls appear in gold.
  5. For a custom lottery, select Custom and enter your own range and ball count.

Common Uses

  • Generating lucky numbers for the weekly UK Lotto or EuroMillions draw
  • Picking Powerball or Mega Millions entries from the US
  • Creating multiple lines for office lottery syndicates
  • Australia Powerball, Irish Lotto, and Canada 6/49 players
  • Teachers demonstrating probability and combinations in maths lessons
  • Quick "lucky dip" alternatives when buying tickets at the last minute

Why Use Spinnit

Numbers are picked using a fair random shuffle — the same method used to deal cards — so every combination is equally likely. The tool covers nine major lotteries plus a fully configurable custom mode. Free, instant, no account required, and works perfectly on mobile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these numbers truly random?

Yes. The generator uses a random shuffle of all possible numbers, giving every combination an equal chance of appearing. No number is more likely than any other.

Can I generate multiple lines at once?

Yes. Set the "Number of lines" field to up to 6 and all lines will be generated together, clearly labelled and separated.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up required.

Does this work on mobile?

Yes, all Spinnit tools are mobile-first and work on any device.
