The basics

What is an AI assistant?

An AI assistant is a computer service that responds to instructions written or spoken in everyday language. You might ask it to explain an idea, draft a message, organise a list or suggest a plan. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are examples.

A prompt is simply the request you give the AI. It does not need special code. Clear everyday language is enough.

AI does not understand or judge information like a person. It produces a likely response based on patterns in data. That response can be helpful, incomplete, out of date or completely wrong—even when it sounds confident.

Think of it as a quick assistant, not an authority

Use AI to help you draft, organise, explore and learn. Keep responsibility for checking the answer and making the final decision.

Everyday AI

Good first tasks for beginners

Start with a low-risk task where you can recognise whether the answer is useful. Avoid beginning with medical, legal or financial decisions.

Make writing clearer

Rewrite a message so it sounds warmer, shorter or easier to understand.

Plan something familiar

Create a packing list, a simple itinerary or questions to take to an appointment.

Understand a topic

Ask for a plain-English explanation, then request an example or a short quiz.

Generate ideas

Explore meals, hobbies, gifts or activities, then choose and check the ideas yourself.

Try it

Your first useful prompt

A helpful request usually contains three things: the task, the relevant details and the shape of the answer you want. You can add or correct information in a follow-up message.

Example prompt

Help me write a friendly email to four friends about a Saturday lunch. Ask whether 12:30 works and whether anyone has dietary requirements. Keep it warm and under 120 words.

This works because the AI knows what to create, who it is for, which details to include and how long it should be. Read the result carefully, change anything that does not sound like you and remove any details you do not want to share.

If the answer is not right

You have not failed. Tell the AI what to change: “Make it less formal”, “Use shorter sentences” or “Ask me two questions before you try again”.

Safety and judgement

Five rules to use AI more safely

  • 01Keep private information private. Do not paste passwords, security answers, payment-card details, account numbers or full personal records into an AI chat.
  • 02Check important facts elsewhere. Confirm health, legal, financial and safety information with a qualified professional or reliable primary source.
  • 03Pause when something feels urgent. Messages demanding money, login details, secrecy or immediate action may be scams. Contact the person or organisation through a trusted route.
  • 04Ask what may be wrong. Try: “Which parts of this answer are uncertain, may be out of date or need checking?” Then do the checking.
  • 05Keep the final decision yours. AI can suggest options, but it does not know your full circumstances and cannot take responsibility for the outcome.

AI services have different privacy settings and data policies. Before entering sensitive material, check the service’s current privacy controls and terms. When in doubt, leave identifying details out.
