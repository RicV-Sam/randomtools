Watch, then try it yourself

Learn one useful step at a time: get started, ask, improve and check. These lessons are designed for complete beginners, including adults over 50. Pause, replay and practise at your own pace.

  2. Open its practice activity and try the steps yourself.
  3. Check what you managed before continuing. Watching alone does not complete the activity.
