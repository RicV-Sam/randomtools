Choose by fit, not hype

ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude: which AI assistant should a beginner try?

There is no single best assistant for everyone. Start free, try the same low-risk task in one or two services and choose the interface, answers and privacy controls you understand best.

Get the quick answerRun a fair test

Quick answer

Start with the service that has the least friction for you

ChatGPT

A sensible first choice if you want OpenAI’s general-purpose chat experience and the widest range of beginner learning material on Spinnit.

Gemini

Worth considering if you already use a Google account and deliberately want an assistant that can work with selected Google services. Review connected-app and activity settings first.

Claude

Worth trying if you prefer its interface or response style, particularly for careful drafting and discussing longer text. Review its consumer privacy settings before sharing content.

All three can draft, explain, brainstorm and organise. The quality varies by task, product updates and the context you provide. None should be treated as automatically accurate.

What matters

Use five questions instead of a feature checklist

  1. 01Can I use the interface comfortably? Check text size, voice options, navigation and whether the service works well on your usual device.
  2. 02Does it handle my real task well? Test a low-risk task you understand rather than a vendor’s demonstration.
  3. 03Can I understand the privacy controls? Find activity, training, history, memory and connected-app settings before adding personal context.
  4. 04Can I verify the answer? Look for clear uncertainty and usable sources, but open and check those sources yourself.
  5. 05Do I need to pay? Use the free access available to you until a specific, repeated need justifies a subscription.

A fair ten-minute test

Give each service the same low-risk prompt

Comparison prompt

Help me plan a relaxed half-day visit to [general place] for two adults. We prefer indoor activities and do not want a rushed schedule. Give three options, explain what I should verify and do not invent opening times or prices.
  • ✓Which answer follows the request without unnecessary detail?
  • ✓Which clearly marks prices, times and availability as facts to verify?
  • ✓Which interface makes follow-up questions easiest for you?
  • ✓Which service’s privacy and history controls can you find and understand?

Do not choose from one impressive answer. Try a writing task and an explanation task on another day before settling on a service.

Before paying

Wait for a repeated need

Plans, limits, features and prices change frequently. A paid plan may offer higher limits or additional tools, but it does not make every answer correct. Before subscribing, write down the limitation you are actually encountering and confirm on the provider’s official pricing page that the paid plan solves it.

Avoid paying because a comparison table says “best”

Choose a subscription only when you can name the task, frequency and feature that make it worthwhile. Review renewal terms and cancel through the same official account or app-store route used to subscribe.

Privacy check

Each provider handles activity and connected services differently

OpenAI offers Data Controls and Temporary Chat. Google provides Gemini Apps Activity and connected-app controls. Anthropic provides privacy and model-improvement settings for consumer Claude accounts. The exact defaults and retention rules can vary by service, plan, region and time.

Whichever service you choose, keep passwords, payment details, security codes and complete private records out of ordinary chats.
