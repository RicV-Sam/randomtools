How did the independent task feel?

A small task for another day

Without copying an example, ask for a short thank-you note to a fictional neighbour who watered your plants. Ask for one change. Check that the assistant did not add gifts, promises or other details you never supplied.

If you need help, repeat the relevant lesson. There is no deadline and no lost streak.

Your take-away checklist
  1. Open the official platform and start a new chat for a fresh task.
  2. Say what you want, add only the details needed, and leave out private information.
  3. Ask for one useful change in the same conversation.
  4. Check facts against your source. Verify real-world claims independently.
  5. Copy the useful part and read it before using it.
For the person helping you test

Let the learner operate their own device. Ask them to say what they expect each button to do. Help when asked, and note where help was needed. Never ask for passwords, private conversations or screenshots of personal information.

  • Could they find ChatGPT, send a message and return to this lesson?
  • Could they make a useful follow-up and spot an unsupported detail?
  • Could they complete the book-group task without a copied prompt?
  • What felt confusing? What would they like to try next?
  • On another day, can they complete the thank-you task with less help?

Keep these observations in your own notes. Nothing is submitted from this checklist. Judge the course by where people need help, rather than treating difficulty as a learner’s failure.

Stuck, or your ChatGPT screen looks different?

I cannot find the message box

Check the address is chatgpt.com. Finish any sign-in screen first. If you see Chat and Work, choose Chat. Look for New chat, sometimes inside a side menu. Screens can vary by device and account; use the official web guide (new tab) if needed.

I lost my conversation

If your account shows recent chats, look there. Otherwise start again with the fictional coffee-morning details. Our browser bookmark saves your place in this lesson, not your ChatGPT conversation.

I cannot paste or copy

On a computer, select the message box and use Ctrl+V (Windows) or Command+V (Mac) to paste. On a phone or tablet, touch and hold in the message box to look for Paste. You can always type the short request instead. To copy a reply, select its text and choose Copy, or use a copy control if shown.

It asks me to sign in, pay, or wait

Use the free-account picture guide (new tab) for sign-up help and a checklist for usage-limit notices. This lesson does not require a paid plan. If free access is unavailable or a usage limit appears, use “Try this task later” and return when you can. Reading the example does not count as completing the practical task.

I am nervous about making a mistake

Use only the fictional details. These steps ask for drafts; you do not need to connect apps, upload files or send a message to anyone. You can stop, edit your next request or start a new chat. If a permission or payment request surprises you, pause and get help.
