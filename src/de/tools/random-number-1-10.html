Picking a random number between 1 and 10 is one of the most common everyday decision shortcuts. It's simple, memorable and fair — each number has exactly a one-in-ten chance. This page gives you that pick in a single tap, with no setup and nothing to install.

Typical uses

  • Picking a winner from a small group of up to ten people
  • Choosing which of ten tasks, chores or prompts to do next
  • Classroom activities — pick a student, a page number, a question
  • Rating prompts for icebreaker games ("rate this from 1 to 10")
  • Settling a small decision when you can't pick between options

How to use

Tap GENERATE or press Space — your number appears instantly. Generate up to 100 at once by changing the count field. The last ten results stay visible for comparison.

Looking for another range?

Need different bounds? Use the main Random Number Generator for any custom range, or jump to 1–100, 1–1000, 1–20, or 1–6.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are both 1 and 10 possible results?

Yes. The range is inclusive — 1 and 10 appear just as often as the numbers between.

How often will each number come up?

Each number has a 10% chance per roll. Over many rolls, the distribution flattens out towards equal — but over a handful, streaks are normal and expected.

Is this better than thinking of a number myself?

Yes. Humans are terrible at picking "random" numbers — most people overchoose 7 and underchoose 1 or 10. A generator is genuinely fair.
