When the dice go missing, a random number between 1 and 6 is the obvious substitute. This generator mirrors a fair six-sided die exactly: each face has a 1-in-6 chance, rolls are independent, and the result is ready the instant you tap the button.

When a 1–6 roll is what you need

  • Board games where you've lost the dice — Monopoly, Ludo, Snakes & Ladders
  • Tabletop RPGs that call for a d6 skill check or damage roll
  • Classroom activities — assign groups of six, pick a question number
  • Drinking and party games that use a single die
  • Coding exercises that need a simple uniform distribution

Need multiple dice?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a fair replacement for a real dice?

Yes — in fact, it's often fairer. Real dice can be biased by uneven weight or worn edges. Digital rolls are mathematically uniform: each number from 1 to 6 has exactly a 1-in-6 chance.

Can I roll two dice at once (2d6)?

Yes. Set "How many rolls?" to 2 and both results appear together. Add them to get the 2d6 total.

Does this work offline?

Yes. Once the page has loaded, all rolls happen in your browser with no network needed.
