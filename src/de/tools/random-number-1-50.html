A range of 1 to 50 is one of the most common mid-size draws. It's big enough to feel like a real draw, small enough that every number feels reachable. Perfect for school bingo, mid-size raffles, and any group of up to fifty people.

Where 1–50 shines

  • School or office bingo (most bingo cards use 1–50, 1–75, or 1–90)
  • Raffle draws at fundraisers and local events
  • Assigning a number to each of up to 50 attendees
  • Half-and-half 50/50 lottery style games
  • Quick probability demos — "pick a number, nobody can guess it"

Draw multiple numbers

For bingo calls or multi-winner raffles, set How many numbers? up to 100. Note that draws are independent — duplicates can appear. If you need unique-only picks (like a real bingo cage), use List Shuffler with numbers 1–50.

Other ranges

Frequently Asked Questions

Are both 1 and 50 possible?

Yes. The range is inclusive — every number from 1 to 50 has a 2% chance on every draw.

Can I use this to call bingo?

For 1–50 bingo style games, yes. For real no-repeat bingo, shuffle a list of 1–50 with the List Shuffler and call them in shuffled order.

Does the generator remember previous picks?

It shows your last ten picks on screen, but won't exclude them. Each draw is statistically independent.
