Safety before shortcuts

Useful answers still need your judgement

  • Do not paste passwords, payment details or private identifying information into an AI chat.
  • Check important facts with a reliable source, especially health, legal and financial information.
  • Treat urgent requests for money, login details or secrecy as possible scams—even if the message looks convincing.
  • Ask what is uncertain, what may be out of date and which sources you should verify.
