Recommended route

Five steps from first question to confident practice

You can open any guide directly. If you would prefer one clear route, follow these steps in order.

  1. Understand AILearn what an assistant can do, where it can go wrong and which first tasks are low risk.
  2. Have a first conversationComplete one useful task and ask a simple follow-up question.
  3. Choose an everyday taskFind a useful, low-risk job in writing, planning, learning or checking.
  4. Protect and checkKeep private information out, verify important facts and pause when something feels urgent.
  5. Build the skillUse the five-lesson course to practise clearer requests and better judgement.
