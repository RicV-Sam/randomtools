Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Spinnit (spinnit.site) is a static random tools website. You do not need an account to use it, and the text you enter into tools is processed in your browser rather than uploaded to a Spinnit database.

What data we collect

Spinnit does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal information to use the tools. Names, class lists, giveaway entries, generated passwords and random results are not stored in a Spinnit account or database.

Some pages may load third-party services from Google for site measurement or advertising. Those services may process technical information such as your IP address, browser, device, page URL and cookie or advertising identifiers, depending on your browser settings and consent choices.

Cookies

Spinnit does not require first-party login cookies because there are no user accounts. Third-party Google services may set cookies or use similar identifiers for measurement, fraud prevention, ad delivery or ad personalisation where permitted.

Google AdSense

Spinnit may display advertisements served by Google AdSense to help keep the site free. Google AdSense may:

  • Set DoubleClick and other advertising cookies in your browser.
  • Use your browsing behaviour to serve personalised ads (where permitted by your consent choices).
  • Collect your IP address, browser type, and other technical identifiers.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Google measurement

Spinnit may use Google measurement tools to understand aggregate site usage, such as which pages are visited and whether the site is working across devices. Spinnit does not use these tools to collect the names, class lists, passwords or random results you enter into the tool interfaces.

Third-party links

Some tool pages may contain links to external websites (for example, Wikipedia articles about countries or names). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external site you visit.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Spinnit is a general-purpose utility site. We do not knowingly ask children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK) to provide personal information, and the tools do not require accounts. Parents, guardians and schools should review this policy and their own browser or network settings when using sites that load third-party services.

GDPR & UK GDPR rights

If you are located in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR, including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you.

Because Spinnit does not run user accounts or store tool inputs in a Spinnit database, there is usually no account record for us to access, correct or delete. If you wish to exercise rights regarding Google's processing of data through its services, please contact Google directly or use their privacy controls.

If you have any GDPR-related queries specific to Spinnit, email us at privacy@spinnit.site and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we change measurement, advertising or privacy settings. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of Spinnit after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions, concerns, or GDPR requests? Reach us at privacy@spinnit.site.
