Workplace random tools

Free decision tools for teams, facilitators, managers and workshop leads. Rotate meeting roles, set standup order, split people into groups, shuffle task lists and make low-stakes team decisions without another app or account.

Use randomness where it belongs: these tools are for low-stakes operational choices, fair turns and transparent group setup. Important decisions about hiring, pay, health, legal issues or performance should use human judgement and the right process.

Which workplace tool should I use?

Meeting Rotation Generator

Rotate facilitator, note-taker, timekeeper, demo order and standup order across recurring meetings.

Random Team Generator

Split people into workshop groups, breakout rooms, project teams or practice teams.

Fair Rotation Generator

Create a no-repeat order for turns, tasks, presentations, office jobs or volunteer slots.

List Shuffler

Shuffle agenda items, presentation order, feedback order, interview slots or task lists.

Poll Maker

Run a quick live vote for workshop priorities, meeting topics, team activities or group decisions.

Random Picker Wheel

Spin a visual wheel for meeting topics, icebreakers, activities, roles, prompts and low-stakes team choices.

Draft Order Generator

Choose a fair pick order for team games, workshop resources, office pools, giveaways and draft-style activities.

Random Question Generator

Generate clean icebreaker questions, retrospective prompts and team conversation starters.

Would You Rather Generator

Generate quick two-choice icebreaker questions for retrospectives, workshops, offsites and team check-ins.

Truth or Dare Generator

Use clean workplace truth prompts for low-pressure team icebreakers, retrospectives and offsites.

Tournament Bracket Generator

Create a knockout bracket for office contests, team games, workshop challenges and friendly competitions.

Decision Maker Tools

Use a coin flip, yes/no wheel or quick picker when the decision is genuinely low-stakes.

Randomness and Fairness

Read the plain-language fairness guide before using random picks in a group setting.

Copy a workplace playbook

Daily standup order

A quick routine for agile teams and recurring check-ins.

  1. Paste team members into Meeting Rotation Generator.
  2. Use the Standup template.
  3. Generate four to eight future standups.
  4. Copy the rota into Slack, Teams or the recurring agenda.

Workshop breakout groups

Use this before training, retrospectives or planning sessions.

  1. Paste attendee names into Random Team Generator.
  2. Choose the number of breakout groups.
  3. Generate and copy the teams.
  4. Paste the groups into the facilitator notes or slide deck.

Fair presentation order

A transparent way to decide who presents first without debate.

  1. Paste presenters into Fair Rotation Generator.
  2. Generate a no-repeat order.
  3. Copy the fairness receipt.
  4. Share the order before the session starts.

Best workplace use cases

Team meetings: rotate facilitator, notes, timekeeper, demo order, retro host and speaking order.

Workshops: split groups, shuffle agenda order, poll priorities, spin a picker wheel, choose presentation order and generate random questions, discussion prompts, clean truth prompts or icebreakers.

Team games: create draft orders and knockout brackets for office challenges, training games and friendly competitions.

Operations: rotate small tasks, office chores, review queues, volunteer turns and on-call warm-up duties where random allocation is appropriate.
