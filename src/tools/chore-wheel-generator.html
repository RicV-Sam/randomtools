This chore wheel generator makes a fair rotating chore chart from your people and task lists. It works for roommates, families, dorms, shared houses and small office spaces. Add the people, add the chores, choose daily, weekly or monthly rotation, then copy the plan.

When to use this chore wheel

Use this when a household, dorm, shared house, family or small office needs a clear chore rota without building a spreadsheet. Add the people, add recurring tasks, then copy a weekly, daily or monthly plan everyone can understand.

How the rotation stays fair

The tool creates a starting person order, then moves each chore one person along in every new period. That means the bathroom, bins, kitchen or dishes do not stay with the same person forever. If you choose a random starting order, the first week is shuffled before the rotation begins.

Good chore wheel setup

  • Keep chore names short so the copied rota is easy to read.
  • Split heavy tasks into smaller tasks if one chore is much harder than the rest.
  • Regenerate only before the rota starts, not after seeing who got which task.
  • Use the Fair Rotation Generator for one-list turns instead of chore assignments.

Chore wheel FAQ

Can this make a roommate chore chart?

Yes. Add each roommate and each recurring chore, then generate a weekly rota. The copy button gives you a plain-text version for WhatsApp, Slack, email or a noticeboard.

What if there are more chores than people?

That is fine. Some people will receive more than one chore in a period, and the assignments rotate in future periods.

Is this a printable chore wheel?

Use your browser print command after generating the rota, or copy the result into a document if you want to style it before printing.
