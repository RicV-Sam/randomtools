This penalty shootout generator creates a random best-of-five football penalty shootout and continues into sudden death if the score is level. Use it for World Cup watch parties, football debates, classroom games, pub quizzes, FIFA/EA FC house rules or quick social posts.

Not a prediction tool

The scoring chance is a simple random setting for fun. It is not based on team strength, player data, goalkeeper stats, betting odds or real match probabilities.

Ways to use it

  • Decide a fantasy knockout tie when friends cannot agree.
  • Run a classroom or youth-club football game without a pitch.
  • Add a quick random result to a football watch party.
  • Generate silly "what if" penalty outcomes for World Cup debates.

Penalty shootout FAQ

Does it include sudden death?

Yes. If the teams are level after five penalties each, the generator continues until one team wins a sudden-death round.

Can I change the scoring chance?

Yes. Increase it for a high-scoring shootout or lower it for more misses. The setting is for fun only.

Can I use real teams?

Yes. Type any two football teams, countries, schools, clubs or fantasy team names.
