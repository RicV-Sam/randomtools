Random Colour Generator

Generate beautiful random colours with all colour codes

Colour history

The Random Colour Generator creates a new colour on every click and gives you the HEX, RGB, and HSL codes ready to copy. You can filter by mood — bright and vivid, soft pastels, or dark tones — making it genuinely useful as a design aid rather than just a random novelty. Your last ten colours are shown in a history strip for easy comparison.

How to Use

  1. Click Generate Colour to get a new random colour.
  2. Choose a colour mode — Any, Bright, Pastel, or Dark — to filter the range.
  3. Click any format row (HEX, RGB, or HSL) to copy that value to your clipboard.
  4. Use the colour history strip at the bottom to revisit any of your last ten colours.
  5. Press Space to generate a new colour without using the mouse.

Common Uses

  • Finding starting-point colours for UI or graphic design projects
  • Generating accent colours for web development work
  • Creating random colour palettes for illustration or branding experiments
  • Educational use in art or digital design classes
  • Colour theory exercises and creative inspiration sessions
  • Picking background colours for social media posts or thumbnails

Why Use Spinnit

Colour codes copy to the clipboard in one click with no ads interrupting the workflow. Three format types — HEX, RGB, and HSL — are shown simultaneously so you always have the value you need. Works offline after first load, with no sign-up ever required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I filter by colour type?

Yes. The colour mode dropdown lets you restrict results to bright/vivid colours, soft pastels, dark tones, or leave it on Any for the full range.

How do I copy the colour code?

Click on any of the three format rows (HEX, RGB, or HSL) and the value copies to your clipboard instantly. A brief confirmation message appears to confirm the copy.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up required.

Does this work on mobile?

Yes, all Spinnit tools are mobile-first and work on any device.
