A random student picker is the calmest way to call on the class. No hands up, no favourites, no guessing who's been quiet — the tool picks, and everyone accepts the result. Paste your roster once, tap once per question, and the "don't pick twice" option makes sure every student gets a turn before anyone gets asked again.

How teachers use it

  • Cold calling — ask a question, let the tool pick who answers
  • Reading aloud — pick who reads the next paragraph
  • Presentations — decide presentation order fairly
  • Starter activities — pick a student to recap yesterday's lesson
  • Behaviour-neutral calling — removes the perception that you're targeting specific students

Projector tips

The picked name is rendered in large text so it reads from the back of the room. For an even bigger display, press F11 to go full-screen. Names entered in the roster are never sent anywhere — the whole tool runs in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my class list saved anywhere?

No. The names live only in this browser tab. Close the tab and they're gone — nothing is sent to a server, and no account is required.

What happens when every student has been picked?

If "Don't pick the same student twice" is ticked, the tool announces the list is complete and lets you tap Reset list to start again from the full roster.

Can I use this for a whole-school assembly?

Yes — there's no limit on the roster length, and the picker runs instantly even on a list of several hundred names.
