Truth or dare safety note

Good truth or dare games should stay voluntary, kind and low-pressure. Skip anything that would embarrass, exclude, reveal private information, damage property or make someone uncomfortable. For group decisions instead of prompts, use the Poll Maker. For two-choice conversation prompts, use the Would You Rather Generator.

Truth or dare generator FAQ

Are these prompts family friendly?

Yes. The prompts are clean and designed for mixed-age use, including kids, classrooms, families and group games.

Can I use only dares?

Yes. Set Prompt type to Dare only. You can also choose Truth only or Truth or dare mode.

Can teachers use this?

Yes, with judgement. Choose the Classroom category and keep prompts voluntary, kind and appropriate for the group.

Does Spinnit save answers?

No. The generator runs in your browser and does not require an account.
