Random letter generator FAQ

Can I generate vowels only?

Yes. Choose Vowels only to pick from A, E, I, O and U.

Can I generate consonants only?

Yes. Choose Consonants only to exclude A, E, I, O and U.

Can letters repeat?

Yes. Turn off unique mode if repeated letters are allowed.
