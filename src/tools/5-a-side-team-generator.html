This 5-a-side team generator creates random football teams from a player list. It also works for 6-a-side, 7-a-side, 11-a-side, PE lessons, training games, Sunday league warmups, office football and casual kickabouts.

Optional ratings and goalkeepers

For a quick random draw, enter one player per line. For balanced teams, add a rating from 1 to 5 after a comma. To mark a goalkeeper, add gk. Example: Alex,5,gk or Sam,3.

Best used for

  • 5-a-side football teams
  • 6-a-side and 7-a-side league nights
  • 11-a-side training games
  • PE lessons and school football
  • World Cup watch-party mini games
  • Office football and charity matches

Fairness note

Ratings are optional and subjective. They help spread strong players across teams, but they are not a ranking system. If you want a pure random draw, untick balanced ratings.

5-a-side team generator FAQ

Can I make 5-a-side teams from 10 players?

Yes. Set the number of teams to 2 and side size to 5-a-side, then generate.

Can teams be balanced by ability?

Yes. Add optional ratings like Ben,4 or Mia,2 and keep balanced mode switched on.

Can it spread goalkeepers?

Yes. Add gk after goalkeeper names. The tool tries to spread them across teams before assigning other players.
