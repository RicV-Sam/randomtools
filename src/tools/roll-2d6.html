Recent rolls

Rolling 2d6 — two six-sided dice — is the most-used roll in tabletop gaming. Monopoly, Catan, Backgammon, Risk and countless RPGs all run on it. The sum ranges from 2 to 12, with 7 as the most common outcome because it has six ways of being rolled (1+6, 2+5, 3+4 and their mirrors).

Games that use 2d6

  • Catan — resource production rolls
  • Monopoly — movement, doubles trigger extra turns
  • Backgammon — movement, with doubles doubled
  • Risk — attack and defence rolls
  • Powered by the Apocalypse RPGs — every action check
  • Traveller and many classic wargames

2d6 probability quick reference

The odds of each total out of 36 possible outcomes: 2 and 12 appear 1/36 (about 2.8%), 7 appears 6/36 (16.7%), with the rest forming a bell curve. That's why 7 is the magic number in so many games — and why Catan robber placement always feels aggressive.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the most common result on 2d6?

Seven — it has a 1-in-6 chance (16.7%). The totals form a triangle: 2 and 12 are rarest, 7 is most common.

Do the dice rolls happen at the same time?

Yes. Both dice are rolled simultaneously and independently, exactly like a real pair of dice thrown together.

Can I tell when I've rolled doubles?

Yes — the two dice values are shown individually above the total, so doubles are easy to spot.
