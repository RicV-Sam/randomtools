    Recent activity picks

      The Random Activity Picker helps answer the everyday question: what should I do? Paste your own ideas, load a preset, or collect suggestions from a group chat and let the picker choose one. It works for boredom, family time, date ideas, weekend plans, screen-free breaks, team activities and low-stakes group decisions.

      How to use the random activity picker

      1. Paste activity ideas into the list, one per line or separated by commas.
      2. Or load a preset such as bored, family activities, date ideas or team breaks.
      3. Leave avoid repeats on if you want every option used once before repeats.
      4. Click Pick Activity and copy or share the result.

      Best used for

      Boredom decisions

      Turn a vague "I'm bored" moment into one clear next action without scrolling forever.

      Family and weekend plans

      Pick from family-safe options, rainy-day plans, weekend ideas or screen-free activities.

      Date ideas

      Paste ideas you would both enjoy and let randomness break the tie for the next plan.

      Team breaks

      Choose a quick workplace break, warmup, lunch pairing or meeting energizer.

      Custom activity list ideas

      Try lists such as free things nearby, date ideas under a budget, rainy-day activities, things to do without a phone, quick team energizers, family games, weekend errands, solo reset ideas or small creative projects. The picker removes duplicates during each pick, and no-repeat mode helps avoid choosing the same activity twice in one session.

      Polls versus a random activity pick

      Use this tool when every option on the list is acceptable and you just want one clear next activity. If people should vote, use the Poll Maker. For food, use the Random Dinner Picker. For movie night, use the Random Movie Picker. For two options, use the Coin Flip or Yes / No Wheel.

      Random activity picker FAQ

      How does the random activity picker work?

      Paste activity ideas or load a preset, then click Pick Activity. The tool randomly chooses one option from the current list in your browser.

      Can I paste my own activity list?

      Yes. Add one activity per line or separate ideas with commas. The picker works with family plans, date ideas, weekend options, team breaks and boredom lists.

      Does this check weather or opening hours?

      No. Only include activities that are realistic, safe and available for your group. The picker chooses from the list you provide.

      Can I avoid repeat activity picks?

      Yes. Turn on avoid repeats this session and the picker will use each activity once before the list resets.
