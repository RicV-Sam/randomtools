      How to use the random picker wheel

      1. Type or paste your options into the box, one per line or comma-separated.
      2. Use a preset if you want a fast lunch, activity, chore, prize, classroom or meeting wheel.
      3. Click Spin the Wheel or tap the wheel itself.
      4. Copy the result, copy the full option list, share the page or use projector mode for groups.
      5. Clear the wheel when using a shared classroom, work or event device.

      Best used for

      Everyday decisions

      Spin for lunch, dinner, chores, date ideas, weekend activities, games, playlists or what to do next.

      Classrooms

      Use the wheel for classroom prompts, review games, rewards, reading turns, activity choices or visible group decisions.

      Workshops and meetings

      Pick icebreakers, breakout leads, meeting roles, agenda topics, demo order or a low-stakes team activity.

      Giveaways and events

      Spin for prizes, door draws, raffle moments, party games, tournament extras or stream-friendly reveals.

      Random picker wheel vs Wheel of Names

      Use this Random Picker Wheel when the options are any kind of choice: activities, prizes, chores, food, prompts, meeting roles or game ideas. Use the Wheel of Names when the list is mainly people. If you need a faster text result, use the Pick a Random Name tool or the List Shuffler.

      Privacy and saved wheels

      Your wheel options are saved in your browser's local storage on the current device. Spinnit does not require an account and does not upload your option list to a server.

      Random picker wheel FAQ

      Can I use this as a spin-the-wheel picker?

      Yes. Add any list of options, click Spin the Wheel, and the picker shows a random result when the wheel stops.

      Can I use names on this wheel?

      Yes. This wheel accepts names too. For a name-focused version, use the Wheel of Names.

      Can I use it on a projector?

      Yes. Use projector mode for classrooms, meetings, live streams, group games and event draws.

      How many options can I add?

      The wheel supports up to 80 options. Shorter lists are easier to read on the wheel, especially on phones and projectors.
