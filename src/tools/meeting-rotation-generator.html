This meeting rotation generator creates a fair rota for recurring team responsibilities. Use it for agile standups, sprint ceremonies, weekly team meetings, workshops, demo order, note-taking, facilitator rotation or any recurring role that should not fall on the same person every time.

Why it is useful for teams

Teams often need a simple standup order generator or meeting role rota, but they do not need project-management software just to decide who facilitates next. This tool creates a fair order in seconds and gives you a copyable result for Slack, Teams, Notion, Google Docs or a calendar description.

High-intent searches this supports

  • meeting rotation generator for recurring team roles.
  • standup order generator for agile and daily scrum meetings.
  • facilitator rotation for workshops and retrospectives.
  • team role rota for note-takers, hosts and timekeepers.

Privacy note

The tool runs in your browser. Team member names are not uploaded to Spinnit, and there is no account or workspace connection. For simple one-list turns instead of role assignments, use the Fair Rotation Generator.

Meeting rotation FAQ

Can this create a standup order?

Yes. Click the Standup template or enter a single role such as "Standup first". The generated table includes a speaking order for each meeting.

Can I rotate multiple roles at once?

Yes. Add roles such as facilitator, notes, timekeeper and demo first. The rota moves each responsibility along the team list for future meetings.

Is this for remote teams?

Yes. Copy the output into Slack, Microsoft Teams, Notion, Google Docs, Confluence or your meeting agenda.
