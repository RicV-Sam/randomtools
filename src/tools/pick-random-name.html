This is the fastest way to pick a random name from a list on the internet. No account, no pop-ups, no 30-second wheel animation — just paste, tap, done. Every name has an equal chance, each pick is independent, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

  • Who goes first — games, presentations, who makes the coffee

  • Research sampling — pick a random respondent from a shortlist

Pick several at once

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the pick really random?

Yes. The tool uses the browser's built-in random number generator, which is unbiased and unpredictable. No name is secretly favoured.

Are duplicates removed from my list?

No — the tool treats every line as a separate entry. If a name appears twice in your list, it has double the chance of winning, which is sometimes what you want.

Can I prove the draw was fair for a public giveaway?

Screen-record the pick or take a time-stamped screenshot. Post it with the final entry list and the date. That's the same level of transparency real draws use.
