    Recent movie picks

      The Random Movie Picker helps answer the classic group question: what should I watch tonight? Paste your watchlist, add suggestions from friends, or load a preset shortlist and let the picker choose one movie at random. It is useful for movie nights, families, date nights, parties, dorm rooms, watch clubs and quick group decisions.

      How to use the random movie picker

      1. Paste movie titles into the list, one per line or separated by commas.
      2. Or load a preset such as family night, comedy, classics or date night.
      3. Leave avoid repeats on if you want every title used once before repeats.
      4. Click Pick Movie and copy or share the result.

      Best used for

      Movie night decisions

      Stop scrolling and choose from the titles everyone already agreed were acceptable.

      Family and kids picks

      Load a family preset or paste a parent-approved list so the result stays easy to say yes to.

      Date night shortlists

      Build a small list of films you would both watch, then let randomness break the tie.

      Group chats and parties

      Collect suggestions, paste them in, pick one movie and share the result back to the group.

      Custom watchlist ideas

      You can paste a streaming watchlist, a film club shortlist, a family-approved kids list, movies leaving a service soon, award winners you missed, comfort rewatches or a list of films friends suggested. The picker removes duplicate titles during each pick, and no-repeat mode helps avoid choosing the same film twice in one session.

      Polls versus a random movie pick

      Use this tool when the group is happy with any movie on the list and just wants a fair choice. If people should vote, use the Poll Maker. If the choice is between two films, use the Coin Flip or Yes / No Wheel. For dinner before the film, use the Random Dinner Picker. For what to do before movie night, use the Random Activity Picker. For conversation before the film starts, try Would You Rather or Truth or Dare.

      Random movie picker FAQ

      How does the random movie picker work?

      Paste movie titles or load a preset shortlist, then click Pick Movie. The tool randomly chooses one title from the current list in your browser.

      Can I paste my own watchlist?

      Yes. Add one movie per line or separate titles with commas. The picker works with your own watchlist, family shortlist, streaming queue or group suggestions.

      Does this check streaming availability?

      No. It does not check Netflix, Disney, Prime Video, Hulu or other live streaming catalogues. Paste movies that are already available to your group if availability matters.

      Can I avoid repeat movie picks?

      Yes. Turn on avoid repeats this session and the picker will use each title once before the list resets.
