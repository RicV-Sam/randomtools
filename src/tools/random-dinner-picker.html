    Recent dinner picks

      The Random Dinner Picker helps answer the daily question: what should I eat tonight? Paste meals, restaurant names, takeout options, leftovers or family-approved choices, then let the picker choose one at random. It is useful for couples, families, roommates, group chats, office lunches, meal planning and nights when nobody wants to decide.

      How to use the random dinner picker

      1. Paste dinner ideas into the list, one per line or separated by commas.
      2. Or load a preset such as weeknight dinners, takeout, family meals or cheap dinners.
      3. Leave avoid repeats on if you want every option used once before repeats.
      4. Click Pick Dinner and copy or share the result.

      Best used for

      Weeknight meals

      Choose from simple dinners you already know how to make when everyone is hungry.

      Takeout decisions

      Paste restaurants or cuisines and let the picker break the tie for a group order.

      Family meal planning

      Build a safe, approved dinner list and use it whenever nobody wants to choose.

      Office lunches

      Pick from lunch spots, delivery options or team meal ideas without a long chat thread.

      Custom dinner list ideas

      Try lists such as five quick meals, restaurants nearby, freezer meals, low-cost dinners, vegetarian options, kids' favourites, meals using ingredients you already have, or takeout choices everyone can agree on. The picker removes duplicates during each pick, and no-repeat mode helps avoid the same dinner twice in one session.

      Polls versus a random dinner pick

      Use this tool when every option on the list is acceptable and you just want a fair choice. If people should vote, use the Poll Maker. If the choice is between two meals, use the Coin Flip or Yes / No Wheel. For what to do before or after dinner, use the Random Activity Picker. For another nightly decision, try the Random Movie Picker.

      Random dinner picker FAQ

      How does the random dinner picker work?

      Paste dinner options or load a preset list, then click Pick Dinner. The tool randomly chooses one meal from the current list in your browser.

      Can I paste my own meal list?

      Yes. Add one dinner idea per line or separate options with commas. The picker works with home meals, takeout choices, restaurant names, leftovers or meal prep options.

      Does this account for allergies or dietary needs?

      No. Only include options that are safe and suitable for the people eating. The picker chooses from the list you provide.

      Can I avoid repeat dinner picks?

      Yes. Turn on avoid repeats this session and the picker will use each dinner option once before the list resets.
