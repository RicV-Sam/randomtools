What each field does

1. Role

Calibrates the AI's expertise, vocabulary, and perspective. Without it, the AI responds as a generic assistant.

2. Task

The primary action. Be specific: "Write", "Summarise", "Analyse", "Generate", "Rewrite". Vague verbs produce vague results.

3. Context

Supplies the who, what, and why the AI needs to give a relevant answer, including what to exclude or avoid.

4. Output

Defines format (table, list, prose), length (word count, item count), and structure (headings, columns, sign-off).
