How to run a fair sweepstake

  1. Write the participant list before generating the draw.
  2. Check the team list before generating the draw.
  3. Choose how many teams each participant should receive.
  4. Generate once, then copy the fairness receipt to the group chat.
  5. Avoid redrawing after seeing which teams people received unless everyone agrees.

What this tool does not do

This tool does not set entry fees, odds, prizes, betting markets or gambling rules. It simply creates a random team assignment for informal sweepstakes and games.

Can I use this for an office sweepstake?

Yes. The copied result works well in email, Slack, Teams, WhatsApp or a printed office notice.

What if I have fewer participants than teams?

Increase teams per participant or leave some teams unassigned. The receipt shows how many teams were assigned and left over.

Can I use it for the Women's World Cup, Euros or AFCON?

Yes. Replace the team list with any tournament field and generate the draw the same way.
