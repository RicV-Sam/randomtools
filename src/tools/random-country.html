Press the button to pick a random country

The Random Country Generator picks one of the world's 195 recognised nations at random. You can filter by continent to focus on a specific region, and each result shows the country's capital city, continent, and population. Great for geography revision, travel inspiration, or just satisfying curiosity about the world.

How to Use

  1. Choose a continent filter from the buttons, or leave it on All for any country.
  2. Click Pick Random Country to get your result.
  3. View the country name, capital city, continent, and population.
  4. Click again to discover another country from anywhere in the world.

Common Uses

  • Geography revision and classroom quizzes on world capitals
  • Travel inspiration when you can't decide where to visit next
  • World history or culture research starting points
  • Pub quiz preparation across nations and capitals
  • "Where in the world?" guessing games with friends or family
  • Daily country-of-the-day challenges for social media or learning

Why Use Spinnit

The full list of 195 nations is built into the tool, so it works offline after first load with no external API calls. Continent filtering means you can focus on a specific region for study or travel planning. Free, instant, and no sign-up required.
