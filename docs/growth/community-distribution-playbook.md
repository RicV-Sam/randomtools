# Community Distribution Playbook (Weekly)

## Goal
Drive qualified discovery for new `/ai/...` pages and gather language from real users to improve CTR and relevance.

## Rule Set
- Post only pages that solve one concrete problem.
- Lead with utility, not promotion.
- Tailor framing per community; do not cross-post identical copy.

## Weekly Cadence
1. Pick 1 newly published page with strongest practical value.
2. Share in 2-3 relevant communities max.
3. Capture questions/objections in scorecard and feed into refreshes.

## Post Template (Value-first)
- Context: "I compared X vs Y for [specific use case]"
- Key takeaway: one clear recommendation with caveat.
- Link placement: one link, after value summary.
- Close: invite corrections or additional edge cases.

## Tracking
Log each share in weekly scorecard:
- Community
- URL
- Angle
- Comments/engagement
- Follow-up content idea
