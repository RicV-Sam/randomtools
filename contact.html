Common questions

Can I suggest a new random tool?
Yes — please do. Most of our best ideas came from user suggestions. Tell us what you'd use it for and any specific features you'd want.

Can I link to Spinnit from my blog / classroom site?
Absolutely. No permission needed. If you do, we'd love to hear about it — drop us a line so we know.

Found a bug?
Include the page URL, what you did, what went wrong, and your browser (e.g. Chrome on iPhone). That's enough to reproduce almost anything.

Press, partnership, or advertising enquiries?
Use the email form with "Press" or "Partnership" in the subject and we'll route it to the right place.
